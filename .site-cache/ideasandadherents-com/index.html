How We Work

The TR Model: A Framework for Success

Our work is guided by the TR Model, a framework that recognises that impact is created and sustained through the right Thoughts and the right Relationships.

T Thought

We help you develop the clarity of thought required for high-impact work: clear problem definitions, sound strategic reasoning, and context-aware decision-making. Without strong thinking, even well-funded projects fail.

R Relationships

We help you build and manage the relationships that make impact possible: internal team alignment, partner and stakeholder coordination, and trust with funders and communities. Without strong relationships, even good ideas stall.

TR in Practice

We intentionally design and manage both the ideas guiding your work and the people and systems supporting it. This is how we help organisations move from activity to enduring impact.

Why Ideas & Adherents

Most failed impact projects do not fail due to lack of passion. They fail due to weak thinking, poor alignment, fragile relationships, and inconsistent execution. Ideas & Adherents exists to be the partner that helps you avoid these failures and build work that lasts.
